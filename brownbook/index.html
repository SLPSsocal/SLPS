Brownbook — Sponsor Directory

Directory of businesses that have sponsored the Surti Leva Patidar Samaj of Southern California golf tournament.

Sources: 2026 sponsor list sent for the tournament slideshow (Sep 17–18, 2026), the tournament app's sponsor records (contacts, phones, emails), and the "2026 Golf Sponsors" sheet (payment history 2022–2026). "Sponsor since" is the earliest year with a recorded payment; the sheet only goes back to 2022, so "2022 or earlier" may be older. A dash means the sponsor is listed but no payment year is on file (often in-kind). Websites are taken from the sponsor's email domain where no site was on file. Service types marked Verify weren't clear from the records.
